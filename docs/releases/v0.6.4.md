This update refines desktop video browsing and playback, and improves comic update handling on desktop and Android.

## Improvement

- Show Bilibili play counts on video covers with compact units, and display publication times using relative labels or calendar dates.
- Show the UP name and publication time together in search results, while omitting repeated UP names in creator upload lists.
- Move the Bilibili danmaku button immediately to the right of Subtitles in the bottom playback controls.
- Make the speed button's active and hover backgrounds smaller while keeping its click target unchanged.
- Dismiss a comic's update notice after finishing its latest chapter or volume, preserve older unread chapters, and sync the dismissal across devices until new chapters arrive.

## Fix

- Prevent Bilibili favorites from appearing as music after switching playback, and repair recognized misclassified records while preserving favorites and playback progress.
