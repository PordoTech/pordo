## Feature

- Check for, download, and install updates from Settings on macOS, with automatic recovery if the new version fails to start.

## Improvement

- Browse Komiic catalogs with tabs for all entries, individual chapters, and volumes.
- Mark a comic chapter as read as soon as you open it.

## Fix

- Keep covers and controls in place while media loads.
- Keep the artist name and cover steady when switching tracks in the music queue.
- Save settings when old macOS Keychain credentials are unavailable, without prompting for Keychain access.
- Keep Bilibili AI detection working when community markers are unavailable or the audio ends slightly early, and show the result in Settings.
