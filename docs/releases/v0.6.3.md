This update focuses on desktop playback and search. Android packages are also rebuilt with the new version.

## Feature

- Optionally skip Bilibili intros and outros using available community markers, with separate switches and colored timeline ranges marking their start and end.

## Improvement

- Browse and switch Bilibili parts as cover cards with names and durations in the shared play-queue sidebar.
- Arrange video controls as quality, speed, subtitles, volume, play queue and fullscreen, hiding quality for local videos.
- Group video-source and AI-subtitle settings under one Video tab, with source settings first.

## Fix

- Keep the display awake while videos play, releasing sleep prevention when playback pauses or ends ([#7](https://github.com/PordoTech/pordo/issues/7)).
- Open the Bilibili play queue even when the local file queue is empty.
- Enter playback immediately after selecting a Bilibili video and cancel pending loading when returning.
- Show search progress inside music, video and comic search buttons, and keep comic-refresh progress at its normal size.
- Retry initial video audio-output failures, defer device recovery while video is paused and keep audio notices on playback pages.
- Keep Bilibili account loading beside account controls and distinguish pending sign-in checks from a signed-out state.
