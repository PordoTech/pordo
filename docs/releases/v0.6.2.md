## Feature

- Watch Bilibili videos with danmaku and adjust its display area, opacity, font size and scrolling speed.
- Play videos at 1.25×, 1.5×, 2.0× or 3.0× while preserving audio pitch.

## Improvement

- Open settings from the upper-right corner of music, video and comic playback pages.
- Give videos more space with a compact control bar and full-width seek bar, removing the ten-second jump buttons.

## Fix

- Return to playback reliably by clicking the current cover after visiting settings.
- Show Bilibili covers in the mini player and playback history.
