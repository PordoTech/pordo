This release refreshes desktop playlist, video update, and tab-badge visuals, refines CJK and Latin typography, and fixes automatic lyrics scrolling.

## Improvement

- Polish desktop music playlists with multi-track collage covers, consistent dark modals, custom favorite selection rows, table headers, hover controls, and floating undo toasts.
- Replace numeric counts on the Comic and Video Updates tabs with a corner dot indicator, and refine creator group headers, status rows, and card actions on the Video Updates page.
- Improve desktop font pairing, weight hierarchy, and lyric spacing, and add a Sync button to jump back to the active line after manual scrolling.

## Fix

- Fix desktop lyrics auto-scrolling so active lines stay centered during playback, pause reliably when scrolling manually, and resume after seeking or switching tracks.
- Fix singular song count labels (`1 song`) in desktop playlist headers, cards, and add-track dialogs.
